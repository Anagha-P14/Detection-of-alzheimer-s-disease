**“Detection of Alzheimer’s disease”**

 Alzheimer’s disease(AD) is a type of neurodegenerative disease also called dementia. It is a condition where neurons within the brain stop functioning, lose connection with other neurons and die. From
 studies it is revealed that it occurs to the people of age 65 to 70.The symptoms of AD include memory loss, difficulty in speaking, degradation in thinking ability and judgement, change in personality
 and behaviour.For a patient with AD, condition will progressively deteriorate over a long period of time. Unless a method of prevention or treatment is discovered.The estimated total cost for
 care of people with AD and other dementias in the US will grow to about 1 trillion in 2050 from an estimated 226 billion in 2015. There are a lot of approaches which have been widely explored,
 focusing on early prediction at personalized level which varies with individual patients and changes over time. Therefore, understanding how AD develops on an individual patient over time is the
 key to enable early prediction of AD and to provide health care services accordingly in an effective manner. Predicting it at an early stage becomes very prominent for the following reasons: • If
 predicted at an early stage then access to different treatment options become easier. • Provides an opportunity to participate in clinical trials.

**AIM**
 The early stage detection of AD is through leveraging heterogeneous medical data mainly through the structural MRI.Along with MRI data, considering the demographics will improve the classification
 accuracy.

The main objective of the project are as follows:
 1. Image acquisition from ADNI dataset.
 2. Separation of data collected as per patient ID.
 3. Pre-processing of MRI scans and feature extraction using Discrete wavelet transform (DWT).
 4. Developing suitable algorithm using machine learning to accurately identify AD.
 5. Applying big data tool Spark to improve the accuracy of prediction.
